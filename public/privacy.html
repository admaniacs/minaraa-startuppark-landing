Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains how iQue Startup Parks Private Limited, trading as Startup Park ("Startup Park", "we"), collects and uses your personal information when you register for Founders, Reset Your Energy — a wellness session hosted by MINARAA at iQue Startup Park, Bengaluru, on Saturday, 10 October 2026 from 8:30 AM on this website.

What we collect

  • Details you enter: name, email, mobile number, company (optional), role, number of passes and your health confirmation.
  • Payment information: handled by Razorpay. We receive only the payment status, payment ID, amount and payment method type — never your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To confirm your booking and send your pass and session information.
  • To contact you about the Session, including any changes.
  • To follow up if you started but didn't complete a booking.
  • To keep records required for accounting and tax.

We don't sell your information. We don't send you unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

  • MINARAA, which runs the Session, for the attendee list and safety.
  • Service providers that operate this booking: Razorpay (payments), Vercel (website hosting), Google (email delivery and our registration spreadsheet).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep registration records for as long as needed for the Session and our legal and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing contact@thestartuppark.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Razorpay's checkout may use cookies needed to process your payment securely.
