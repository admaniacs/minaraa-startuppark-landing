Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy applies to passes for Founders, Reset Your Energy — a wellness session hosted by MINARAA at iQue Startup Park, Bengaluru, on Saturday, 10 October 2026 from 8:30 AM, sold by iQue Startup Parks Private Limited (Startup Park).

All sales are final

  • Once your registration is confirmed, passes are non-refundable. This includes cancellations by you and not attending.
  • Passes are non-transferable. Each pass is issued in the attendee's name and can't be given or resold to someone else.
  • Please check your details and the number of passes before you pay.

If we cancel or reschedule

  • If we cancel the Session, you get a full refund.
  • If we move it to another date, you can keep your pass for the new date or ask for a full refund.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay.
  • Where a refund applies, we start it within 2 working days. It usually reaches your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • If a payment failed but money was deducted, it is reversed automatically within 5–7 working days. Contact us if it doesn't arrive.
